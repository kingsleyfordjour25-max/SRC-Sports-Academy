About SRC Sports Academy

Building skills, developing talents and inspiring champions.

Who We Are

SRC Sports Academy is a sports development platform dedicated to encouraging participation in sports, developing athletic abilities and promoting physical fitness. We provide opportunities for individuals to discover their talents, improve their skills and develop confidence through sports.

Our academy embraces different sporting disciplines and promotes teamwork, discipline, determination and respect. We believe that sports can help individuals develop both physically and mentally.

Our Mission

To create opportunities for individuals to develop their sporting talents through quality training, teamwork and continuous improvement.

Our Vision

To become a recognised sports academy that inspires excellence, promotes healthy living and develops talented and responsible athletes.

Our Objectives

  • Identify and develop sporting talents.
  • Promote physical fitness and healthy living.
  • Encourage discipline, teamwork and leadership.
  • Provide opportunities to participate in competitions.
  • Make sports accessible to people with different abilities.

Our Core Values

Discipline

Encouraging commitment, consistency and responsibility.

Teamwork

Building cooperation and positive relationships.

Excellence

Striving for continuous improvement in every activity.

Respect

Promoting fairness and respect for everyone.
